Important Academic Dates

  • Jan 11: Semester begins.
  • Jan 18: Martin Luther King Jr. Day, non-instructional day.
  • Feb 15: Presidents' Day, non-instructional day.
  • Mar 15-19: Spring recess, no class.
  • Mar 26: Prince Kūhiō Day and Good Friday, non-instructional day.
  • May 5: Last day of instruction.
  • May 6-7: Study period.
  • May 10-14: Final examination period.
  • May 14: Semester ends.
