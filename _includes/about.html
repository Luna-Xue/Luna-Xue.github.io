About

Xiaochan (Luna) Xue is a tenure-track Assistant Professor in the University of Hawaiʻi at Mānoa. Her research lies at the intersection of wireless communication, cybersecurity, and artificial intelligence. Her group develops trustworthy AI-native wireless systems by bridging physical-layer security, ISAC sensing, and learning at the RAN edge. Recent interests include privacy-preserving and resilient AI-RAN/O-RAN prototyping, defenses against adaptive and generative AI threats, and ToM-inspired network agents for intent-aware multi-stakeholder decision making.

Research Areas

Intelligent Wireless Systems: Networking, Sensing, and IoT
AI-native wireless networking and ISAC for intelligent IoT, focusing on AI-RAN architectures, real-time sensing–communication integration, and closed-loop system intelligence.
Security, Privacy & Trust
Secure and privacy-preserving FutureG wireless intelligence, including AI-RAN/O-RAN security, ISAC-enabled sensing, confidential computing, federated learning, and robust edge AI.
AI & Language Model & Agents
Machine learning for electronic design automation, SLM/LLM-based Verilog generation, multi-agent systems for chip design and mathematical problem solving.
