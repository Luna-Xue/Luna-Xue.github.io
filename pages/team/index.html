About WINGS Lab

The Wireless Intelligent Networks for FutureG with Security Lab develops trustworthy AI-native wireless systems at the intersection of wireless communication, cybersecurity, and artificial intelligence.

Our work bridges physical-layer security, integrated sensing and communication, learning at the RAN edge, and practical AI-RAN/O-RAN prototyping. Current directions include privacy-preserving and resilient wireless intelligence, defenses against adaptive and generative AI threats, and intent-aware network agents for multi-stakeholder decision making.

Research Areas

Intelligent Wireless Systems: Networking, Sensing, and IoT
AI-native wireless networking and ISAC for intelligent IoT, with AI-RAN architectures, real-time sensing-communication integration, and closed-loop system intelligence.
Security, Privacy & Trust
Secure and privacy-preserving FutureG wireless intelligence, including AI-RAN/O-RAN security, ISAC-enabled sensing, federated learning, and robust edge AI.
AI & Language Model & Agents
SLM/LLM-based design automation, multi-agent systems, and AI agents for wireless, chip-design, and mathematical reasoning workflows.

Facilities

AI-RAN Testbed
AI-RAN/O-RAN testbed with USRP X310/X410/B210, OAI, O-CUDU, Open5GS, GPU servers, and 5G NR FR1/FR2 mmWave support.
Jetson Orin NX Super 16GB
Embedded edge computing platform for smart-car control and AI-RAN experimentation.
NVIDIA RTX PRO 6000
Four NVIDIA RTX PRO 6000 Blackwell Max-Q Workstation Edition GPUs for AI-native wireless, cybersecurity, and edge AI research.
ROSOrin Pro ROS2 Smart Car
Robotics and sensing platform with ToF LiDAR, 3D depth camera, ROS/Orin Pro, WiFi cards, and 5G communication kits for wireless security and ISAC prototyping.
