This project studies adaptive mode selection for mmWave integrated sensing and communication systems. The work focuses on spectrally disjoint composite waveforms, sensing-communication tradeoffs, and dynamic system adaptation under changing wireless conditions.

Research Focus

  • Adaptive mmWave ISAC mode selection
  • Spectrally disjoint composite waveform design
  • Tradeoff analysis between sensing and communication
  • Experimental validation under realistic wireless conditions
