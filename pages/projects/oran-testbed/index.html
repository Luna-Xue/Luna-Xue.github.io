This project develops an O-RAN and AI-RAN testbed for secure, intelligent, and programmable wireless networking. The testbed supports experimentation with RAN telemetry, control loops, AI-driven decision making, and security analysis.

Research Focus

  • O-RAN and AI-RAN system integration
  • Near-real-time control and telemetry collection
  • Wireless network intelligence and automation
  • Security, reliability, and resilience of programmable RAN systems
