This project explores RIS-assisted and O-RAN-enabled sensing for intelligent UAV detection. The work investigates how programmable wireless environments and RAN intelligence can support sensing, detection, and adaptive wireless control.

Research Focus

  • RIS-assisted wireless sensing
  • O-RAN-enabled sensing and control
  • UAV detection and tracking
  • AI-assisted sensing adaptation in dynamic environments

Related Publication

Publication: ROISD: RIS and O-RAN Assisted Intelligent Sensing for UAV Detection, IEEE Annual Congress on Artificial Intelligence of Things (AIoT), 2025.
