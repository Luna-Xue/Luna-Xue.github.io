This Physical AI project studies remote robotic control as a network-aware
physical system. In the UHM Vertically Integrated Project (VIP) setting, students
use a smart robot car, a steering console, live sensing feedback, and the WINGS
O-RAN/AI-RAN testbed to explore how wireless network behavior affects embodied
AI and real-time control.

### Project Focus

- O-RAN-assisted remote robot driving and physical-system control
- Sensing feedback through RGB, IR, depth, and AI-assisted perception views
- Network-aware control loops that connect RAN telemetry with robotic behavior
- Hands-on Physical AI research through the UHM VIP project structure

### Infrastructure Connection

The project uses the O-RAN/AI-RAN testbed as infrastructure for command delivery,
video feedback, telemetry collection, and future xApp-based control policies. The
robotic platform provides a concrete Physical AI workload where wireless latency,
coverage, congestion, and sensing quality can directly influence control decisions.
