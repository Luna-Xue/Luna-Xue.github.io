This project investigates reconfigurable intelligent surfaces for programmable wireless environments. The work explores how RIS platforms can reshape propagation, support wireless sensing, improve communication reliability, and interact with AI-RAN control loops.

Research Focus

  • RIS-assisted sensing and communication
  • Programmable wireless propagation control
  • mmWave and near-field wireless experimentation
  • AI-assisted configuration and real-time adaptation

Related Publication

Publication: ROISD: RIS and O-RAN Assisted Intelligent Sensing for UAV Detection, IEEE Annual Congress on Artificial Intelligence of Things (AIoT), 2025.
