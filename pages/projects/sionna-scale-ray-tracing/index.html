This project develops Sionna-enabled large-scale ray tracing workflows for AI-RAN and integrated sensing and communication research. The work focuses on measurement-calibrated urban RF modeling, wireless digital twins, and simulation pipelines that connect realistic propagation behavior with AI-native RAN control and ISAC evaluation.

Digital Twin Workflow

The workflow combines scene-level geometry, semantic environment information, and Sionna-based ray tracing outputs. This makes it possible to connect physical layouts and object classes with channel behavior, sensing coverage, and RAN decision-making policies.

Research Focus

  • Large-scale Sionna ray tracing for urban wireless environments
  • Measurement-calibrated RF channel and propagation modeling
  • Wireless digital twins for AI-RAN control and optimization
  • ISAC evaluation under realistic geometry, mobility, and blockage conditions
